About Law & Legacy

Law & Legacy is a pre-eminent, multidimensional law chamber in Bangladesh. Our practice is predominantly litigation based, providing comprehensive advice to individuals, companies, banks, small businesses, government entities and multinational corporations.

We understand the complexities that arise in a rapidly growing economy. Our team blends courtroom experience with practical advisory, offering solutions from documentation to litigation.

Why Clients Choose Us

  • Experienced advocates trained in top universities in Bangladesh and abroad.
  • Strong documentation practice — land verification, registrations, licensing and regulatory filings.
  • Proven track record across high-profile matters and complex multi-party disputes.
  • Client-first approach with responsive service and pragmatic strategy.
